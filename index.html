About

I am an applied researcher interested in how public programs affect health, education, and economic opportunity. I hold an M.P.A. in Economic and Financial Policy from Cornell University and an M.S. in Public Policy from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I currently work in finance and operations at the International Institute for the Brain (iBRAIN) in New York City, and I am preparing for doctoral study in economics.

Research

Community Health Centers and Mortality Revisited: Heterogeneity-Robust Evidence from a Staggered Rollout
Independent research, 2026

Replicates Bailey and Goodman-Bacon (2015, AER) and re-estimates the effect of Community Health Centers on mortality using heterogeneity-robust difference-in-differences estimators (Sun–Abraham, Callaway–Sant'Anna, Goodman-Bacon decomposition, HonestDiD). The direction of the published effect is robust, but its magnitude depends on the estimator, population weighting, and the cohort of centers studied.

Public Education Resources and Educational Opportunity: Evidence from the Housing Market
Master's thesis, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2020

Studies how the allocation of public school resources is capitalized into housing prices in Shanghai, and what this implies for equity in access to education.
